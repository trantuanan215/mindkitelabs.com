Services

Five capabilities, one goal: an organization that runs faster and more accurately.

01

Digital transformation

Build a roadmap based on real readiness, choose tools that fit the budget and make the most of existing systems.

Example: move expense approvals out of chat groups into one approval flow with history and deadlines.

  • Digital maturity assessment
  • Phased 6–18 month roadmap
  • Digitized forms, approvals and records
  • Vendor-neutral software selection
02

Data & BI

Agree on metric definitions, bring data from many sources into one place and build dashboards leadership actually uses every morning.

Example: end-of-day revenue and inventory reports that update automatically instead of being compiled by hand late into the evening.

  • KPI dictionary
  • Data warehouse and automated pipelines
  • Executive, sales and operations dashboards
  • Data quality and access governance
03

Applied AI

Pick the right work for AI, keep a human reviewer and measure the error rate. No AI just for the sake of AI.

Example: an assistant that answers new staff questions on internal policies and procedures, with citations to the source documents.

  • AI use case screening and prioritization
  • Internal knowledge assistant
  • Document extraction and feedback classification
  • Demand and inventory forecasting
04

Operating processes

Write the process down before automating it: who does what, when, and how it is measured, so the system does not depend on a few individuals.

Example: standardize the order-to-delivery process and attach a processing-time KPI to each step.

  • Current and target process maps
  • SOPs and RACI matrices
  • Operating KPIs by department
  • Automation of repetitive tasks
05

Education transformation

We work with schools, training centers and corporate learning teams to digitize academic operations, make use of learning data and bring AI into teaching with proper oversight.

Example: track attendance and learning results on one dashboard and flag at-risk learners early so teachers can step in.

  • Digital admissions, academic records and tuition
  • Attendance and learning outcome dashboards
  • AI assistants for lesson planning and grading, with teacher review
  • Digital and AI skills training for staff

Delivery process

Seven steps, one closed loop. It ends with measurement, not handover.

Each step has concrete deliverables for you to approve before moving on.

Step 01

Ideation — research and define the problem

Interview the people doing the work, observe the real process and review existing data. The goal is to find the true bottleneck, not a wish list.

Deliverables
Problem map and opportunities ranked by impact and effort
Typical duration
1–2 weeks

Step 02

Design — target process and solution

Map the target process, data model and interface prototypes. End users review and comment from the first sketch.

Deliverables
To-be process maps, data model, tool mockups
Typical duration
1–2 weeks

Step 03

Validation — agree on scope, cost and KPIs

Leadership and user representatives review the design together. We measure a baseline so results can be compared after rollout.

Deliverables
Agreed scope, baseline KPIs, risk assessment, go/no-go decision
Typical duration
3–5 working days

Step 04

Development — build in short cycles

Work in two-week cycles with a demo on real data at the end of each. Changes are logged and reprioritized openly.

Deliverables
Dashboards, automation flows, AI models, updated SOPs
Typical duration
2–8 weeks

Step 05

Testing — run in parallel with the old way

Reconcile numbers between the new system and the current way of working, and check permissions and edge cases before switching over.

Deliverables
UAT sign-off and resolved issue log
Typical duration
1–2 weeks

Step 06

Deployment — roll out team by team

Role-based training, a gradual department-by-department switch and close support in the first week. Every system has an internal owner.

Deliverables
Live system, user guides, trained internal owners
Typical duration
1–2 weeks

Step 07

Re-evaluation — measure results, start the next loop

After 30, 60 and 90 days, KPIs are measured again against the baseline. New issues become input for the Ideation step of the next improvement loop.

Deliverables
Impact report against baseline, improvement backlog for the next loop
Checkpoints
30 / 60 / 90 days — back to step 01

Step 07 loops back to step 01: digital transformation is continuous improvement, not a one-off project.

FAQ

Questions organizations often ask before getting started.

Does a small business need AI right away?

Usually not yet. Clean data and written-down processes pay off sooner. AI delivers once those two foundations are in place.

Do we have to buy new software?

No. We make the most of the tools you already use, such as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Excel or your accounting software, and only recommend new tools when there is a clear reason.

How is our data kept secure?

We sign a confidentiality agreement before accessing any data, grant only the minimum access needed, never use client data to train external models, and comply with applicable personal data protection laws.

Who runs the system after handover?

Each system has a trained internal owner and user documentation. You can choose the monthly partnership if you want continued support.
